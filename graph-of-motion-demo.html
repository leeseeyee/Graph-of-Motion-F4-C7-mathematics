Form 4 Mathematics · Chapter 7: Graphs of Motion

The graph is not a map of where the car goes.

A distance-time graph shows how far the car is from its start — it is not a picture of the road. Drive the car along a flat, straight track and watch the graph rise and fall, even though the car never leaves the ground.

START HERE A distance–time graph only tells you one thing: how far the car is from where it started, at each moment. It is not a picture of the road. That's why the line can go up and down even though the car only drives left–right on flat ground. Lots of students read the graph like a map by mistake — drive the car below and catch the difference yourself. 👇
